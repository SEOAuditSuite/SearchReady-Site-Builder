What belongs in a careful check?

  • Brakes: response, pad wear and safe adjustment
  • Shifting: cable tension and gear engagement
  • Wheels and tires: condition, pressure and alignment
  • Drivetrain: wear, lubrication and basic cleaning
  • Fasteners and contact points: a safety-first review

Every bicycle is different. A specific service scope and any replacement parts must be agreed after inspection—this demo does not offer pricing or appointments.

BEFORE YOU START

Common questions

How do I know it needs attention?

Rubbing brakes, hesitant shifting, unusual noises or an uncomfortable feel are good reasons to request a mechanical check.

Will every part need replacing?

Not necessarily. A responsible inspection separates adjustment from wear-related repairs and explains the difference.
