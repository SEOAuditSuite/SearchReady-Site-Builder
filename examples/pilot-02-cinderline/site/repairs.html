02 / PROBLEM SOLVING

Find the issue. Then find the right fix.

Good repair work begins with listening: to the rider, to the bicycle, and to the evidence found during inspection.

01

Braking trouble

Weak stopping feel, squealing or a rubbing wheel can have different causes. Start by describing exactly when it occurs.

02

Drivetrain friction

Skipping chains, reluctant shifts and drivetrain noise call for a check of adjustment and component wear.

03

Wheel & tire concerns

Repeated flats, wobble or uneven wear deserve an inspection before choosing a repair or replacement.

04

Unexpected noises

Details about speed, terrain and pedaling help narrow the issue. A remote description alone cannot diagnose safety.

Safety note: If brakes, steering or wheels may be unsafe, stop riding until the bicycle is properly inspected.
