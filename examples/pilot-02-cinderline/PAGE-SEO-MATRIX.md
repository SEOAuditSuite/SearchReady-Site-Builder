**Publication gate:** A verified domain, business identity, service region, real contacts/hours and approved real form delivery/retention are needed before production indexability, canonical URLs, sitemap and relevant structured data can be released. Staging `noindex` is a pass condition for this pilot, not a missing SEO feature.
