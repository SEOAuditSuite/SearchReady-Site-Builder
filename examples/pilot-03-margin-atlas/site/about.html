ABOUT / THE PROJECT

Curiosity is a public practice.

Margin Atlas is a fictional editorial publication created to demonstrate a content-first, search-aware website—not to present a real magazine or a credentialed urban-design authority.

M·A

What belongs here?

Short, original notes that begin with ordinary observations: a street corner, a bench, a threshold. Each piece is designed to be readable without registration, a mailing list, or external scripts.

The demo explores a different website model from a service-business homepage. Article headings, useful internal links, a journal index and lightweight reading interactions all matter here.

What we will not claim

We do not invent people, qualifications, reviews or location facts. The publication is fictional. This staging build is intentionally excluded from search indexing and has no production canonical URL.
